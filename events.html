Sacred Fasting Tithi

Ekadashi Observance Rules

Ekadashi occurs twice every month (on the 11th lunar day of waxing and waning moon). Fasting on this day purifies sins and accelerates devotion to Lord Sri Krishna.

🌱 Permissible Foods (Anukalpa)

  • All fresh fruits, fruit juices, dried fruits, and nuts.
  • Milk, fresh homemade paneer, yogurt, and pure cow ghee.
  • Potatoes, sweet potatoes, raw bananas, and ginger.
  • Buckwheat (Kuttu), water-chestnut flour (Singhare ka atta), and sabudana (tapioca).
  • Rock salt (Sendha Namak), black pepper.

🚫 Foods to Strictly Avoid

  • All grains: Rice, wheat, barley, oats, corn, millet.
  • All beans and pulses: Dal, peas, soybeans, lentils, chickpeas.
  • Mustard seeds, sesame seeds, and hing (asafoetida).
  • Onions, garlic, mushrooms, and non-vegetarian food.
  • Fast is to be broken (Parana) the next morning during specific shastra hours.
