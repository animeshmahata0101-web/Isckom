Daily Aarti & Darshan Schedule

The deities are worshiped according to strict Pancharatrika Vaishnava standards established by Srila Prabhupada. Devotees and visitors are warmly invited to attend all ceremonies.

Mangala Aarti

Most auspicious dawn ceremony awakening the Deities with conch, sweet kirtan, and lamp offering.

Followed by Tulasi Aarti at 5:00 AM
07:15 AM 👑

Shringar & Guru Puja

First darshan of the day in fresh royal silk attire, fragrant flower garlands, and Srila Prabhupada Guru Puja.

Full Altar Open
08:00 AM 📖

Srimad Bhagavatam

Daily morning philosophical discourse elucidating the timeless verses and realizations of Srimad Bhagavatam.

Spiritual Class & Q&A
12:30 PM 🍚

Raj Bhoga Aarti

Opulent midday feast offering to Sri Sri Radha Madhava with 56 devotional preparations.

Altar Reopens

Founder-Acharya's Vision

Carrying the Timeless Message of Pure Love of God

In 1965, at the age of sixty-nine, Srila Prabhupada journeyed from Kolkata across treacherous seas on the steamship Jaladuta to America, carrying only seven dollars and a chest of sacred Srimad Bhagavatam translations. Within a decade, he inspired a worldwide spiritual renaissance, establishing 108 temples, writing over 70 books, and opening the floodgates of Krishna consciousness to the entire world.

Authentic Book Distribution

Distributing Bhagavad Gita As It Is and Srimad Bhagavatam across Jhargram and rural Bengal.

Food for Life Relief

Distributing pure sanctified vegetarian meals to schools, villages, and pilgrims daily.

Every Sunday Evening • All Are Welcome

Join the Sunday Love Feast in Jhargram

Started by Srila Prabhupada in 1966, the Sunday Love Feast is a weekly spiritual festival combining soulful kirtan, enlightened Vedic philosophy, question-and-answer interactive discussions, and a sumptuous feast of pure vegetarian Krishna-Prasadam.
