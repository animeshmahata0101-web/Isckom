Founder-Acharya of ISKCON

His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda

"He built a house in which the whole world could live." Born in Calcutta in 1896, Srila Prabhupada fulfilled the ancient prediction of Lord Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu by taking the chanting of the Holy Names to every town and village across the globe.

The Historic Transatlantic Crossing

Aboard the Steamship Jaladuta (1965)

On August 13, 1965, just days before his sixty-ninth birthday, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami boarded the freighter Jaladuta at King George's Dock in Calcutta. The vessel carried only freight and one elderly passenger traveling in service of his spiritual master, Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Goswami Maharaja.

During the thirty-five-day voyage across the Arabian Sea and the Atlantic, he suffered severe seasickness and survived two massive heart attacks. On the thirty-sixth night, Lord Krishna appeared to him in a dream, urging him forward and assuring His protection.

"My dear Lord Krishna, You are so kind upon this useless soul, but I do not know why You have brought me here. Now You can do whatever You like with me. But I guess You have some business here, otherwise why would You bring me to this terrible place? ... How will I make them understand the message of Krishna consciousness? I am very unfortunate, unqualified and the most fallen. Therefore I am seeking Your benediction so that I can convince them, for I have no power to do so on my own."

— Markine Bhagavata-dharma (Written at Boston Commonwealth Pier, September 18, 1965)

Arriving in New York City with no financial backing, no acquaintances, and no institutional organization, he began chanting under a tree in Tompkins Square Park. In 1966, he founded ISKCON. By the time of his disappearance in 1977, he had circled the globe fourteen times, established 108 temples, initiated over 5,000 disciples, and authored dozens of authoritative translations of Vedic classics.

Constitutional Mandate

The Seven Purposes of ISKCON

Formulated by Srila Prabhupada when incorporating ISKCON in New York on July 13, 1966.

1

Systematic Spiritual Knowledge

To systematically propagate spiritual knowledge to society at large and to educate all people in the techniques of spiritual life in order to check the imbalance of values in life and to achieve real unity and peace in the world.

2

Krishna Consciousness

To propagate a consciousness of Krishna (God), as it is revealed in the great scriptures of India, Bhagavad-gītā and Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.

3

Brotherhood & Oneness

To bring the members of the Society together with each other and nearer to Krishna, the prime entity, thus developing the idea within the members, and humanity at large, that each soul is part and parcel of the quality of Godhead.

4

Sankirtana Movement

To teach and encourage the sankirtana movement, congregational chanting of the holy name of God, as revealed in the teachings of Lord Sri Caitanya Mahaprabhu.

5

Holy Places of Pastimes

To erect for the members and for society at large a holy place of transcendental pastimes dedicated to the personality of Krishna.

6

Simpler & Natural Way of Life

To bring the members closer together for the purpose of teaching a simpler, more natural way of life through cow protection, organic farming, and spiritual community.
