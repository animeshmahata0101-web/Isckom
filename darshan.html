Daily Deity Darshan

"One who sees the Deity form of the Lord with devotion and love becomes purified of all material contamination." — Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam

Presiding Deities

Sri Sri Radha Madhava

Shringar Darshan • Morning Royal Attire

Sri Sri Radha Madhava shower their eternal transcendental mercy upon all devotees visiting Jhargram. Adorned in silk and fragrant fresh tulasi and garland offerings.

Sponsor Garland
Pancha-Tattva

Sri Sri Gaura Nitai

Inaugurators of Sankirtana Movement

Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu and Lord Nityananda Prabhu, arms raised in pure divine ecstasy, distributing the supreme treasure of Krishna Prema freely to every living soul.

Offer Bhoga
Lord of the Universe

Sri Sri Jagannatha Deva

Baladeva & Subhadra Maharani

With large lotus eyes overflowing with unbounded compassion, Lord Jagannatha bestows liberation on anyone who beholds His transcendental face and accepts His Mahaprasadam.

Rathayatra Seva
Protector of Devotees

Lord Sri Narasimhadeva

Half-Man Half-Lion Avatar

Worshiped for protection from all material and spiritual impediments. Daily lamps and fragrant tulasi leaves offered for the protection of the congregation.

Narasimha Kavacha
Founder-Acharya

Srila Prabhupada

Guru-Parampara Acharya

Daily Guru Puja is performed with loving devotion every morning at 7:15 AM, offering arati, flowers, and homage to our beloved eternal spiritual master.
