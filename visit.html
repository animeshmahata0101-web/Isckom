Travel Guide

How to Reach the Mandir

🚆

By Train (Jhargram Railway Station - JGM)

Jhargram is well-connected on the Howrah-Nagpur-Mumbai South Eastern Railway line. Regular express trains like Ispat Express, Steel Express, and local MEMU/EMU passenger trains arrive daily from Howrah (Kolkata) in approximately 2.5 hours.

Station Distance: ~2.5 km (10 mins by Toto or Auto-rickshaw).
🚌

By Bus / Road (NH-49 & State Highway)

Frequent SBSTC and private express buses operate from Kolkata (Esplanade), Kharagpur, Medinipur, Bankura, and Purulia directly to the Jhargram Central Bus Stand.

Bus Stand Distance: ~2 km (Easily accessible by local transport).
🚗

By Personal Vehicle / Taxi

Drive via NH-16 (Kolkata-Kharagpur Expressway), take the NH-49 cutoff at Lodhasuli toward Jhargram. Ample safe parking is available inside the temple campus.

Sanctum Sanctified Behavior

Temple Etiquette Guidelines

👟

Footwear

Please remove shoes, sandals, and socks at the designated free footwear counter before stepping onto the temple parikrama and altar hall.

👗

Modest Attire

As a sacred place of worship, modest traditional or respectful clothing covering shoulders and knees is appreciated.

📸

Photography

Photography of the gardens and temple exterior is welcomed. Please do not use flash or take photos inside the altar hall during Aarti ceremonies.

🌿

Sanctified Prasadam

All food offered to the Deities is strictly vegetarian (no onion, garlic, or meat). Devotees are requested not to bring outside food into the temple.
